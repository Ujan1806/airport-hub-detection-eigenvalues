Mathematical Foundation & Intuition
A v = λ v  ⇔  v_i = (1 / λ) ∑_(j ∈ N(i)) v_j

The centrality score of airport i is proportional to the sum of importance scores of all airports j directly connected to it. Connecting to major hubs confers significantly greater structural prestige than connecting to isolated airports.

Perron-Frobenius Theorem: Since the airport network is connected (irreducible) and non-negative, the dominant eigenvalue λmax is guaranteed to be real and positive, with a strictly positive eigenvector v > 0.

Key Mathematical Discovery from Network:

  • Ahmedabad (AMD) vs Guwahati (GAU): Both airports have exactly 2 direct flight connections. Yet AMD scores 0.3950 while GAU scores 0.2778 (+42% boost) because AMD connects directly to India's top two hubs (DEL and BOM).
  • Kochi (COK) vs Kolkata (CCU): Both have 3 connections. COK achieves 0.5657 (+35% over CCU's 0.4195) by strategically bridging BOM, BLR, and MAA.
