Mathematical Formulation & Core Insights
W v = λ v  ⇔  λ v_i = ∑ W_ij v_j
An airport's score v_i is proportional to the sum of scores of its neighbors v_j weighted by the daily flight volume W_ij.
Perron-Frobenius Guarantee
Because the flight network is connected (irreducible) and non-negative, the dominant eigenvalue λmax is strictly real and positive, guaranteeing strictly positive hub scores (v > 0).
Structure of the Space: Rank & Nullity
For the 10×10 binary matrix A, λ5 = 0. By the Rank-Nullity Theorem: Rank(A) = 9, Nullity(A) = 1 (9 + 1 = 10).
